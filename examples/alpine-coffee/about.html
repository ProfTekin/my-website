Our imagined story

Rooted in

Alpine Coffee is a fictional neighborhood café created to show how a small business website can feel personal, useful and easy to explore.

A place to reconnect.

In our imagined café, people stop for conversation after a walk or settle in with a book. A clear menu and welcoming atmosphere are the heart of the concept.

Made for learning.

This site uses semantic HTML, one shared stylesheet and a short JavaScript file. Its illustrations are original graphics, stored locally. There are no tracking services or external dependencies.
